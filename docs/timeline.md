#Project Dispatch Decision Timeline (non-technical)#
Our original idea for our IBM Z datathon was to create an AI model that in real time directs police vehicles to the area of a crime before the emergency services are called using past data to predict where crime would occur next.
This idea would have been a good solution for Theme 1: Real time AI for critical decisions because choices have to be made at each moment on where to send police based upon a large dataset.

Why not:
Ethical issues - previously overpoliced areas would become more policed, police surveillance of an area can escalate problems and a quicker response times are not as necessary as with ambulances.

New Idea:
Reducing ambulance response times and routing
