The Implementation

Our target client base is an emergency medical dispatch organisation, therefore we believe it is necessary to include all the statistics.

Data

Real NYC 911 medical calls, plus weather, Census and hospital data, joined by ZIP zone and half-hour.

Forecast

LightGBM predicts critical and other calls in every ZIP zone for the next 30 minutes to 3 hours.

8 modelstested on all of 2022, against three baselines

Idle units move toward the zones where demand is expected, at least one unit stays in every borough, routes follow real streets.
